By Alex Vance (Growth & SEO Director) • April 20, 2025 • 7 min read

Step-by-step masterclass on flagging fake competitor reviews, leveraging platform dispute policies, and burying negative ratings under authentic 5-star positive feedback.

### The Devastating Cost of a Single 1-Star Review Studies indicate that **a single 1-star review can cost a small-to-medium enterprise up to 22% of prospective customers**. Three negative reviews can push customer loss past 59%. Competitor attacks, disgruntled former employees, and algorithmic misunderstandings can suddenly jeopardize years of hard-earned goodwill. --- ### Step 1: Formal Platform Removal Requests Both Google Business Profile and Trustpilot provide formal dispute mechanisms for reviews violating terms: - **Conflict of Interest:** Reviews left by competitors or employees. - **Irrelevant / Off-Topic Rants:** Reviews discussing political issues or unrelated events. - **Defamatory or Offensive Language:** Harassment or private information leaks.
